Sign Up Online

Start your AI journey by registering with your APU student email. Create your member profile and get access to workshops, coding sessions, and AI competitions. Once registered, you’ll be part of a growing community passionate about artificial intelligence and innovation.

Participate in Projects

Take part in our AI hackathons, data science bootcamps, and research initiatives held across APU. Whether you’re new to coding or building machine learning models, every activity helps you grow your skills and collaborate with peers.

Stay Connected and Learn

Receive updates about new AI events, talks from industry experts, and upcoming training sessions. Stay active in our Discord and GitHub communities to build, learn, and share your work with others.
